Memi remembers the little things.

Your sister mentioned a book in March. Your friend can't eat hazelnuts. You promised your dad a day out at the coast. Tell Memi, the elephant who never forgets, and it'll keep them in order and bring them back when the birthday comes round.

How it works

Tell Memi about the people you care about. Each of them gets a few categories, and when something comes up, Memi files it in the right one.

Categories are per person

Everyone starts with Gift ideas, Plans and Notes. Add Books for one friend, Recipes for another. Rename or reorder them whenever you like.

Hit or miss

When an idea has been used, mark how it went. Next year, Memi will remind you the candle was a miss.

Your phone, computer and browser, in step

Turn on sync and pick your Google account. Everything is encrypted on your device before it leaves, and kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only your Memi can open. Changes from your other devices arrive within seconds, and edits made in two places are both kept. To add a device, start on it, then tap Allow on one you already use. We never see any of it, because there's nothing of ours in between.

No account

There's nothing to sign up for. Sync is optional and uses the Google account you already have.

What it isn't

It's deliberately small.

Not a contact list

There's no field for addresses or phone numbers. It's for the useful, specific things: what they'd like, what you've planned, what to avoid.

Not a stand-in for the friendship

Memi won't nudge you to message people or score how often you talk. It just remembers what you tell it, so you can show up with the right thing.

Download

Free, open source, and the same Memi everywhere. The newest version is always on the releases page.

Android

Phones and tablets, Android 8 and newer.

Download the APK .apk

Obtainium installs updates straight from our GitHub releases. Don't have it? Get it from obtainium.imranr.dev, then tap the badge again.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11. The installer updates itself in place when you install a newer one.

Download the installer .msi

The installer isn't code-signed. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. Smart App Control, if you have it switched on, blocks unsigned installers completely.

Linux

Most distributions, on X11 or Wayland. Updaters like Gear Lever keep the AppImage up to date.

Download the AppImage x86_64

In your browser

Nothing to install: Memi on any computer, Mac included, or a phone that isn't Android. It keeps everything in that browser, and syncs like the apps if you turn sync on.
