Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

We store zero user data. Memi has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. What you write stays on your own devices, plus your own Google Drive if you turn on sync, and that copy is encrypted so only your devices can read it.

Who we are

Memi is an independent, open-source project. “We” means the people who make it. You can read every line of the code on GitHub, You can reach us at hello@yourmemi.app, or open an issue on GitHub.

Sync (optional, off until you turn it on)

Sync keeps your phone, tablet and computers in step by storing one file in your own Google Drive. This is how it works:

  • The first device to turn on sync creates a random 256-bit key. Before anything leaves your device, it's compressed and encrypted with that key (AES-256-GCM).
  • The encrypted file goes in Google Drive's hidden app data folder. It doesn't appear among your Drive files, and Memi can't see your other files. We only ask Google for access to that one folder (the drive.appdata permission).
  • The key never leaves your devices in a readable form. On Android, Google's Block Store backs it up end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock, so a new phone set up from your old one can pick it up. To add another device, the new device makes a one-off code and shows it (and leaves it in the same hidden folder as a request); a device that already has the key encrypts it for that one device and passes it through the hidden folder, where the new device collects and deletes it. Both devices show the same six-digit code so you can check before you allow it. You can also copy the recovery key by hand.

Because the file is encrypted, Google can't read your notes, names or photos. Google can see that the file exists, its size, when it changed, and which Google account it belongs to. The app shows you the email address of the account you connected, and only on your own device.

Memi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In plain terms: the only thing the app does with your Google access is read and write its own encrypted file.

The web app

memi.yourmemi.app is the same app in your browser, made of static files served by Cloudflare. Everything you add is kept in that browser's own storage and nowhere else, unless you turn on sync, which works exactly as described above. Google's sign-in script is only loaded once you open Sync (or if sync is already on), so a browser that never uses sync never contacts Google. Google gives the web app access to its hidden Drive folder for about an hour at a time, so it may ask you to reconnect. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything the web app kept there.

Google services the Android app uses

On Android, sync relies on parts of Google Play services that run on your phone:

  • Google sign-in, to give the app access to its hidden Drive folder.
  • Block Store, to back up the sync key end-to-end encrypted, as described above.
  • Google code scanner, only when you use Add a computer. It reads the QR code on your phone, and Google says it doesn't store the images or results. Like other parts of Google's ML Kit, it may send Google basic device information and performance diagnostics.

These are only used once you turn on sync, and they're covered by Google's privacy policy. If you never turn on sync, the app never contacts Google or anyone else.

What we never do

  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting, in the app or on this website.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking libraries.
  • No selling, sharing or renting data. We don't have any to share.
  • No connections to anything except Google, and only for sync: accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com.

Permissions

Android: internet access, used only for sync, and permission to check whether you're online (requested by the Google Play services libraries that sync uses; it can't see what you do online). If you turn on birthday reminders, the app asks to show notifications, and it's allowed to set its once-a-day check again after your phone restarts. The app doesn't ask for your camera, contacts, location, microphone or storage. Photos come through Android's photo picker, which only shares the one photo you pick.

Linux: the AppImage is a normal program running as your user. Its data is in ~/.local/share/yourmemi, the sync key goes in your desktop's keyring, and photos come through your desktop's file chooser.

Windows: a normal desktop app installed just for your user account. Its data is in %APPDATA%\YourMemi.

Downloads and updates

The app is downloaded from GitHub, and Obtainium or an AppImage updater checks GitHub for updates. Those services may log your IP address when you download, under their own privacy policies. We don't get those logs.

This website

yourmemi.app is a set of plain pages: no cookies, no analytics, no fonts or scripts from anywhere else. If you use the light/dark button, your choice is remembered in your own browser and never sent anywhere. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security under its own privacy policy. We only see totals, like the number of visits; we can't see who visited. Email sent to us is kept only as long as needed to reply.

Deleting your data

  • On a device: remove a person from inside the app, or uninstall the app (on Android, clearing its storage does the same). In the web app, clear the site's data in your browser.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive's settings, choose Manage apps, find Memi (it may still be listed as Your Memi) and choose Delete hidden app data. Turning off sync in the app also stops it using Drive.
  • Google access: you can remove Memi's access at any time from your Google account's connections page.

Children

Memi isn't aimed at children under 13. We don't collect anything from anyone, of any age.
